arXiv: 2608.09238 · 2026-08-10 · Linzhou Li, Tianjia Shao, Kun Zhou
[EN] Recent monocular 3D face reconstruction methods achieve high fidelity by fitting a 3D Morphable Model (3DMM) to dense priors predicted by networks, but the optimization stage is computationally expensive, often taking tens of seconds per image. We present RealDenseFace, a real-time optimization-based 3D face reconstruction method with dense UV-space network predictions. Our key idea is to formulate 3DMM fitting as a nonlinear least-squares problem and solve it with a tailored Gauss-Newton solver that converges in only a few iterations. The reconstruction is conducted in two stages. In the first stage, the network predicts two dense UV-space maps from a single RGB image: a correspondence map for UV-to-image alignment, and a relative-depth map for geometric constraints along the viewing direction. In the second stage, the solver fits per-vertex targets sampled from these maps at the vertex UV coordinates. The solver supports all three reconstruction settings: single-image fitting, offline sequence reconstruction, and online tracking. Our method achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on the NeRSemble SVFR benchmark. The online tracker runs at 80+ FPS, and the offline sequence reconstruction is over 20 times faster than previous optimization-based baselines.

SFDATrack: Generalized Source-Free Domain Adaptive Tracking Under Adverse Weather Conditions
Siyuan Yao, Ziqi Wang, Ruiqi Yu, Junqi Huang, Wenqi Ren, Xiaochun Cao2026-07-01 (submitted 2026-06-30)
This paper addresses the challenge of maintaining reliable visual tracking in adverse weather conditions (fog, rain, snow, haze) without access to the original source-domain training data. The proposed Source-Free Domain Adaptive Tracking (SFDA-Track) framework operates exclusively on target-domain footage. The architecture features a mean-teacher backbone equipped with Dual Interactive Mamba layers that extract weather-resilient features through bidirectional selective scanning. A hyperspherical prototype projection mechanism maps these features into a shared latent space, ensuring robust adaptability across diverse climate conditions. This approach eliminates the need for source data while achieving competitive tracking performance in degraded visual environments.
本文解决了在恶劣天气条件（雾、雨、雪、霾）下保持可靠视觉跟踪的挑战，且无需访问原始源域训练数据。所提出的无源域自适应跟踪（SFDA-Track）框架完全基于目标域数据进行自适应。架构采用配备双重交互式Mamba层的均值教师骨干网络，通过双向选择性扫描提取抗天气特征。超球原型投影机制将特征映射到共享潜在空间，确保在多种气候条件下的鲁棒自适应能力。该方法无需源域数据即可在退化视觉环境中实现有竞争力的跟踪性能。

This paper presents an integrated system for aerial visual tracking that addresses the critical challenge of generating large-scale annotated datasets without manual labeling. Kai Luo, Fei Teng, Mengfei Duan, Wanjun Jia, Xu Wang, Hao Shi, Kunyu Peng, Zhiyong Li, Kailun Yang2026-06-29
This paper presents a cost-effective approach to multi-object tracking using only point annotations (center markers) instead of full bounding boxes. The system, called PS-Track, shifts emphasis from physical perimeters to central topology. SUMO presents a training-free, zero-shot approach to improving object tracking and segmentation of moving objects. Mohamad Alansari, Yonathan Michael, Hasan AlMarzouqi, Muzammal Naseer, Naoufel Werghi, Sajid Javed2026-06-23
This paper re-examines memory updating in SAM2-based visual tracking, discovering that relying solely on confidence scores for mask selection causes drift during occlusions, fast movements, and when distractors appear. The team presents SENTRY, a training-free "refine-before-write" module that verifies memory updates using neighbor-aware cycle-consistent matching. It collects multiple segmentation candidates per frame, traces them into short tracklets, and applies cycle-consistency to prioritize geometrically and temporally aligned masks. When added to five baseline models, SENTRY improves performance across nine benchmarks, setting new zero-shot state-of-the-art on LaSOT and GOT-10k while maintaining ~33 FPS with minimal memory overhead.

Mohamed Nagy, Naoufel Werghi, Jorge Dias, Majid Khonji2026-06-22
Traditional MOT pipelines depend on fixed visual descriptors that lack robustness and demand heavy computation. Polycepta reformulates visual modeling as a continuous recursive estimation problem, constructing and refreshing independent appearance states for each tracked object.
